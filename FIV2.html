before you land

The whole rulebook

It's short on purpose. Mods enforce it evenly, including on regulars.

Be kind. No exceptions, no "it was a joke."Bigotry, harassment, and creepy behavior get you removed, not warned.

Keep public channels SFW.This includes pfps, names, and status. It's a hangout, not a hookup server.

Ask before DMing anyone.No unsolicited DMs, ever. Report them and we handle it.

Leave other servers' drama at the shore.No callouts, no raids, no dragging fights in from elsewhere.

If something feels off, ping a mod.You'll never be in trouble for reporting something in good faith.

questions people actually ask

Before you knock

Do I have to be a femboy?

No. Trans, enby, questioning, cis, allies — if you're friendly and you follow the rules, you're welcome. Nobody's checking anything at the door.

Is this the same as Femboy Universe?

Same team, different island. Femboy Universe is the main server with VRChat and Minecraft attached. This one is Discord-only and smaller on purpose — the vibe is quieter here. You're welcome in both.

Do I need a mic or camera?

No. Text-only is completely normal, and plenty of people sit in VC muted just for the background noise.

Is it moderated?

Yes, actively. There's a mod team across time zones, a report channel, and no tolerance for creeps. Rules apply to everyone equally, staff included.

Can I be staff?

Applications open in the server when we need people. The realistic answer: be around for a while, be helpful, and you'll get noticed.

The door's already open

No application, no vouching, no waiting on approval. Click, read the rules, come say hi.
