What is Gemma?

Gemma is the bot running quietly in the background of this server. She greets people, hands out roles, takes staff applications and opens support tickets. Most of what she does happens without you asking — this page covers the parts you can actually use.

Every server is different. Server owners choose which features to switch on, so some of the commands below may not exist where you are. If a command doesn't appear when you type /, that feature simply isn't enabled.
Reference

Commands you can use

Type / in any channel and Discord will show you what's available. These are the ones open to everyone.

CommandWhat it doesWhere

/ticket close [reason]Close your own ticket when you're done.Inside your ticket

Replies to these are usually ephemeral — only you see them, and they vanish when you dismiss them. Nobody in chat sees you poking around.

Doing things

Applying for staff

If the server takes applications, there's usually a panel with a dropdown. Pick one, or run /apply — same thing.

  1. Pick what you're applying for

    Moderator, admin, whatever the server has set up. Anything marked closed isn't taking applications right now.

  2. Answer the questions

    Typed questions come as a form (a few at a time), multiple-choice ones as a dropdown. Only you can see any of it. You can cancel at any point and nothing is saved.

  3. Check your answers

    At the end you get the whole thing back to read over. Start over re-does it; Submit sends it.

  4. Wait for a DM

    Staff review it and you'll get a message with the outcome — plus any note they leave. If your DMs are closed you won't get it, so consider opening DMs from server members while you wait.

One at a time. While an application is pending you can't send another for the same role. If you're turned down, some servers set a waiting period before you can re-apply — Gemma will tell you exactly when you're eligible again.
Doing things

Opening a ticket

A ticket is a private channel between you and the staff team. Use it for anything you don't want to say in public — reporting someone, appealing something, or just asking a question quietly.

Press the button on the server's ticket panel, or run /ticket open. A new channel appears and you'll be pinged in it. Say what you need; someone will pick it up.

While it's open

Only you, the staff team and Gemma can see the channel. A staff member may claim it (so two people don't answer at once) or bring in someone else who can help. Attachments and screenshots work normally.

When it's done

Either you or staff can close it with the Close button or /ticket close. The channel disappears about ten seconds later, staff keep a transcript for their records, and you get a DM confirming it closed.

Save anything you need before closing. The channel is deleted, not archived — if there's a link or file in there you want, grab it first.
Doing things

Getting roles

Most servers post a message listing roles with an emoji next to each one. React with the emoji to get the role.

Taking your reaction off removes the role again. Some menus work differently — your reaction disappears the instant you add it, and reacting a second time toggles the role back off. Either way the message itself tells you which.

If nothing happens

The usual reasons: the role sits above Gemma in the server's role list (a server owner has to fix that), the menu needs a role you don't have yet, or the server has blocked that menu for a role you do have. When it's one of the last two, Gemma removes your reaction and DMs you a one-line explanation — so check your DMs before assuming she's broken.

Some servers also lock certain role menus so you can only pick one option at a time, or so a choice can't be undone. That's deliberate, not a bug.

Doing things

The skullboard ☠️

A hall of fame for messages that deserve it. React to a message with ☠️ and if enough people agree, it gets reposted in the skullboard channel with a jump link back to the original.

GemmaBOTToday at 2:14 AM
☠️ 7 | #general
someone said something unforgivable
…and now it lives here forever.
↗ Jump to message

How many skulls it takes is up to the server (five is common). The count on the board updates as people add and remove reactions. Whether your own skull counts on your own message is also a server setting — some allow it, some don't.

Doing things

Welcomes & autoresponses

Two things Gemma does at you rather than for you.

Welcome messages

When you joined, that greeting with your avatar and the member count was her. Nothing for you to do — but if you'd rather not be pinged in one, that's a conversation for the server's mods, since it's their template.

Autoresponses

If you say something that matches a phrase the server has set up, she'll answer automatically — usually FAQ answers, rules links or a joke someone thought was funny. She never replies to bots, and there's a cooldown so a trigger can't be spammed.

Help

Common questions

I reacted for a role and nothing happened.
Check your DMs first — if you weren't allowed, she'll have told you why. Otherwise it's almost always the role being positioned above Gemma in the server settings, which only a server owner can fix. Ask a mod to run /rolemenu view; it flags exactly this.
Can I edit my application after submitting?
No. Ask staff to deny it and re-apply, or explain the correction in a ticket. Before submitting, Start over lets you redo the whole thing.
Who can see what I write in a ticket?
You, the server's support role, anyone staff explicitly adds, and Gemma. When the ticket closes, a transcript of it is saved to a staff-only channel — so treat it as "private from other members", not "private from staff".
Gemma DMed me — is that real?
She DMs about applications, ticket closures and role menu problems only. She'll never ask for your password, a login code, or a link to anything. If a "Gemma" DM asks for any of that, it isn't her — report it.
Why did she ignore my command?
Either that feature isn't enabled, or the server has restricted that command through Discord's own integration settings. /ping is a quick way to check she's alive at all.
Her status says "Error! check logs!"
Something needs attention on the bot's side. Some things may still work fine — the server's owner gets notified automatically, so no need to report it unless it lingers.
Gemma DMed me about a warning / timeout / ban.
That's the server's moderators using her to act — she just delivers the message and the reason. Appeal to the server's staff, not to the bot.
I got banned by a channel I posted in.
Some servers run a trap channel for spam bots, clearly labelled as one, where posting removes you automatically. If that was you and you're a real person, appeal to the server's staff — Gemma just enforces what they configured.

What she remembers

Short version: what you send her on purpose, and what your server configures. Not your general chat.

Applications
Your answers, who you are, and the accept/deny decision — kept so staff can look back at them.
Tickets
A transcript of the ticket channel, saved to a staff channel when it closes.
Roles & settings
Which emoji maps to which role, channel choices, and so on — server configuration, not personal data.
Skullboard
The message that made the board and how many skulls it has.
Everyday messages
Read in the moment to check for autoresponder triggers, then gone. Nothing is stored.

If you want something removed, ask the server's owners — it's their server and their data. They can delete applications, tickets and skullboard entries.
