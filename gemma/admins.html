For server owners

Add Gemma & give her room to work

One invite, then two things to check. Neither takes a minute, and skipping the second causes most of the "it doesn't work" reports I get.

  1. Invite her

    Use the Add to server button above. The link asks for Administrator, which covers everything eighteen features could need: posting, embeds, reactions, managing roles and channels, kicking, banning, timeouts, reading the audit log so logs can name who did what, and the voice permissions for temporary channels.

    If you'd rather not grant Administrator, you don't have to. Invite her anyway and then give her a role with exactly these: View Channels, Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files, Read Message History, Add Reactions, Use External Emojis, Manage Messages, Manage Roles, Manage Channels, Kick Members, Ban Members, Timeout Members, View Audit Log, Connect, Speak, Move Members. Features whose permissions are missing say so instead of failing quietly, so you can trim and see what breaks.

    Administrator is not a hierarchy override. Gemma still cannot touch roles positioned above her own, so step 2 matters just as much either way.

  2. Move her role up

    Server Settings → Roles, drag Gemma's role above every role she'll hand out — autorole, reaction role menus, all of it. Discord won't let any bot manage a role positioned above its own, and this is the single most common cause of "the reaction did nothing".

  3. Check she can see the channels

    If you use private categories, make sure she can view and post in the channels you're about to point her at. She'll tell you when she can't — every command that takes a channel checks first and refuses with the reason.

Commands not showing up? Press Ctrl+R in Discord. New slash commands are cached by the client and a reload is usually all it takes.

Your first ten minutes

This order avoids backtracking. Everything is optional; do the features you want and ignore the rest.

  1. /setup

    Set the log channel first (a staff-only channel — most features report there), then whatever else you're using: welcome channel, skullboard channel, autorole, tickets category and support role, applications review channel.

  2. /welcome test

    Fires a real welcome for you so you can see the default. Change it with /welcome edit.

  3. /application create name:Moderator

    Then add questions, /application preview to walk it yourself, and /application panel to post the picker.

  4. /ticket type create name:Support

    Then /ticket panel wherever members should press it.

  5. /rolemenu create

    Then /rolemenu add once per emoji → role pair.

  6. /staff add @Mods, then the protection checks in /setup

    Tell Gemma who your team is, then open /setup: Jail, Anti-nuke, Anti-raid and Anti-spam each show a status line and a one-click Enable (default) / Build the jail button. Defaults are tuned for set-and-forget.

  7. /logging setup #server-logs

    Every log category to one private channel. Split them later if you like.

  8. /honeypot create — last

    Makes a clearly-labelled trap channel and arms it. Do this after everything else, and move the channel to the bottom of your list.

Text prefix & aliases

Every command in the Moderation section works two ways: as a slash command (/ban) and as a text command with your server's prefix, ,G by default (,G ban @user reason). Text commands are faster for mods and let you attach screenshots straight to the message. Mentioning Gemma works as a prefix too.

CommandWhat it doesWho
/prefix set new_prefix · view · resetChange the text prefix (1-8 characters, e.g. ! or g?), see it, or go back to ,G. Case doesn't matter — ,g ban works.Manage Server
/alias add name command [preset]Make a shortcut. preset is text tacked onto whatever you type — a ready-made reason, for instance. /alias add name:spam command:ban preset:Spam bot makes ,G spam @user the same as ,G ban @user Spam bot.Manage Server
/alias remove name · listDelete a shortcut, or see them all.Manage Server
,G commandsLists the text commands the person running it can use, with their usage.Anyone

Aliases only apply to text commands — slash commands can't be renamed. An alias can't shadow a real command, and whatever you type after the alias still goes through, so ,G spam @user was posting links becomes ,G ban @user was posting links Spam bot.

Protection

Staff roles & jail

The rest of the protection stack leans on these two. One tells Gemma who your team is. The other gives you somewhere to put someone while you sort it out.

Staff roles

Anyone with mod-level Discord permissions already counts as staff. /staff add extends that to roles that don't carry permissions — helpers, trial mods — so anti-spam, anti-raid, the honeypot and the jail never act on them, and so the jail channel is visible to them.

CommandWhat it doesWho
/staff add role · remove role · listManage the staff role list (also a step in /setup).Manage Server

Jail

Jailing strips someone's roles, gives them a Jailed role that can see only #jail (which staff can also see), and remembers everything so /jail release puts it all back. If they leave and rejoin, the Jailed role is reapplied. Timed jails release themselves.

CommandWhat it doesWho
/jail setupCreate (or repair) the Jailed role and #jail, and hide every other channel from it. New channels are hidden automatically afterwards. The Jail step in /setup checks this and offers to build it.Manage Server
/jail add user [duration] [reason]Jail someone — 30m, 2h, 1d, or open-ended. They're DMed and #jail gets a note.Timeout Members
/jail release user [reason]Let them out and restore the roles they had.Timeout Members
/jail listWho's in jail, by whom, until when.Timeout Members

Guardrails: staff can't be jailed without being removed from the staff roles first; nobody above Gemma's role or above yours can be jailed. Anti-raid can use "jail" as its action once the jail exists.

Protection

Anti-nuke

For the day an admin account gets compromised, or a rogue bot gets added. Mass bans, kicks, channel and role deletion, webhooks, emoji deletion, new bots, dangerous permission grants: all counted per person, and whoever crosses the line gets stopped.

Only the server owner and anti-nuke admins can change these settings. Not Manage Server, not Administrator. If an admin's account is taken over, the attacker can't just switch it off. Give trusted co-owners /antinuke admin add; give trusted bots /antinuke whitelist add.
CommandWhat it doesWho
/antinuke enable [mode]default (3 of most things in 60s; bots and dangerous perms trigger on the first), strict (first hit for nearly everything), or custom (keep your own thresholds). Also a one-click step in /setup.Owner / AN admin
/antinuke set module threshold secondsCustom rule for one module, e.g. ban 2 30. Switches mode to custom.Owner / AN admin
/antinuke module module on|offTurn a single module off (say, channel creation on a server that makes lots).Owner / AN admin
/antinuke punishment ban|kick|stripWhat happens to whoever trips it. Default ban.Owner / AN admin
/antinuke whitelist add|remove|list · admin add|remove|listNever punish these users/bots · let these users configure anti-nuke.Owner / AN admin
/antinuke log channel · disable · viewWhere triggers are reported (also DMs the owner) · off · the full config.Owner / AN admin

Modules: mass ban, mass kick, channel deletion, channel creation, role deletion, role creation, webhook creation, emoji deletion, bot added, dangerous permission grant (Administrator, Ban, Kick, Manage Server/Roles/Channels/Webhooks, Timeout, Mention Everyone), member prune. Bots added without being whitelisted are kicked on sight and the person who added them is counted. Gemma needs View Audit Log to know who did what — /antinuke view warns if she can't.

Catches the four things people actually do: too many messages too fast, the same message repeated, one message that's a chunk repeated over and over ("yousuckyousuckyousuck"), and mass mentions.

/antispam log channel · disable · viewWhere cleanups and timeouts are reported · off · the config.Manage Server

When it fires it deletes the offending messages (and that person's other messages from the last 30 seconds in that channel), applies the timeout, drops a short note in the channel that self-deletes, and logs it with a sample. Staff are never touched. Elongated words like "lmaoooo" are deliberately not treated as spam.

Cross-server

Ban network

Run several servers? Link them and a ban in one lands in all of them. You must own every server you link. Gemma verifies it, so nobody can attach your community to someone else's ban button.

CommandWhat it doesWho
/network create [name]Starts a network and gives you a code. Keep it private — it's for your other servers, not for sharing.Server owner
/network join codeLinks this server. Refused unless the same person owns both servers.Server owner
/network direction send|receive|bothsend shares this server's bans but takes none. receive is the opposite. both is the default.Server owner
/network dm-names visited|allWhether the ban DM names every linked server or only the ones that person was actually in. Default: only the ones they were in.Server owner
/network info · leaveWho's linked and what's synced · unlink (bans already applied stay).Manage Server

Only manual moderator bans sync. Honeypot, anti-raid and anti-nuke bans stay in the server that made them — one server's false positive shouldn't become everyone's. Unbans sync too, so lifting a ban anywhere lifts it everywhere.

The banned person gets one DM, not one per server: "You have been banned from Server A and its linked servers Server B, Server C, and Server D." The reason carries across as "Network ban from Server A — [reason] (proof in Server A)", since evidence files stay in the server where they were uploaded.

Cross-server

Global ban list

An opt-in list of confirmed automated accounts and accounts that friend-add members to solicit them. Curated by the bot owner, every entry with a reason and evidence. Your server can join it, override any entry, or leave at any time.

Two switches, and only one of them is the protection. Watch joins is continuous — a listed account that joins gets a DM with the appeal link, then a ban. Sweep is a one-off pass over members already in your server. Enrolling turns watch on; sweeping is always something you choose to run.
CommandWhat it doesWho
/gban enrollStart using the list, watch-joins on. Also a one-click step in /setup.Manage Server
/gban watch enabledThe continuous protection. Turning it off leaves you enrolled but unprotected, and Gemma says so.Manage Server
/gban sweep · sweep-stopCheck everyone already here. Shows how many would be banned and warns about other anti-nuke bots before anything happens, then paces itself and can be stopped mid-run.Manage Server
/gban lookup user_idIs this account listed, why, in what category, when, and is your server ignoring it?Manage Server
/gban ignore add user_id reason · remove · listAllow an account here regardless of the list. Gemma then asks whether you think it's a false ban and, if so, points you at the appeal server.Manage Server
/gban view · unenrollCurrent settings · stop using the list. Bans already applied stay — unban individually if you want them back.Manage Server
/gban unban-pardonedUndo bans here for accounts that have since won an appeal.Manage Server

Before you sweep

A sweep bans several accounts in a row. If you run another protection bot with anti-nuke, it may see that and restrict Gemma — whitelist her there first. She paces herself deliberately (a couple of seconds between bans) to reduce the risk, tells you how long it'll take, and stops on command.

What gets an account listed

Automated/bot accounts, and accounts that immediately friend-add members to solicit them, including NSFW solicitation. Nothing else — this list is not for interpersonal disputes. Every entry needs evidence, and every listed account can appeal at the appeal server; a successful appeal clears them everywhere.

Your own staff are never banned by the list, and neither is your server owner.

Protection

Moderation

Ban, kick, timeout, warn, purge. Every action becomes a numbered case in your mod log carrying the reason, the moderator, and whatever proof you attached.

Set the mod log first (/mod log #channel or /setup) and make sure Gemma can attach files there. Proof screenshots are re-uploaded into that channel so they never expire; without it, cases still work but evidence has nowhere to live.

Attaching proof

With the text prefix, just attach screenshots to the command message: ,G ban @user spamming + drag in the images. With slash commands, use the proof options (up to three per command; add more later with /case proof). Everything is copied to the mod log and linked from the case.

Applications

Staff applications without leaving Discord. A panel to pick from, questions answered privately, and Accept / Deny buttons for your team.

How it runs

A member picks an application from the panel (or runs /apply) and answers privately — typed questions arrive as forms, up to five at a time, multiple-choice ones as dropdowns. They review everything before submitting. The submission posts to your review channel with buttons; whoever decides can attach a note, and the applicant gets a DM either way.

Roles stay in your hands. Accepting doesn't grant anything — Gemma reminds the reviewer to assign roles themselves. Deliberate: promotions shouldn't happen because someone fat-fingered a button.

Autorole

One role, applied the moment someone joins. Deliberately strict about when it fires, for reasons that become obvious the first time a bot backfills 400 members.

Only on joins she sees

If she was offline when someone joined, nothing is applied retroactively. No member sweeps on startup, ever.

Never re-added

Once a mod removes the role, it stays removed. Only a fresh join triggers it again.

Screening-aware

With Membership Screening on, the role lands right after that member finishes the rules gate.

Loud about problems

Deleted role, or one she can't reach? It goes in your log channel instead of failing quietly.

Who can do what

Manage Server

Every configuration command on this page. A moderator with only kick/ban won't have them — grant Manage Server if you want your mods configuring the bot.

Anti-nuke: owner only

Anti-nuke settings can only be changed by the server owner and users on the anti-nuke admin list — never by Manage Server or Administrator alone. That's what makes it useful against a compromised admin.

Moderation commands

Follow Discord's own permissions — Ban Members for ban/unban, Kick Members for kick, Timeout Members for timeout/warn/cases, Manage Messages for purge. If a mod can do it by hand, they can do it through Gemma.

Reviewer & support roles

Per-application reviewer roles and per-type ticket support roles let staff do the day-to-day work without Manage Server over everything.

Everyone

/apply, /ticket open, /ticket close in their own ticket, and the status commands.

Fine-tuning

Server Settings → Integrations → Gemma lets you restrict any individual command to specific roles or channels, on top of everything above.

Checks run on the bot's side, not just in the Discord UI — someone can't get around a restriction by guessing a command name.

Troubleshooting

Commands don't appear when I type /
Press Ctrl+R in Discord — new commands are cached by the client. Also check the invite included the commands scope, and look in Server Settings → Integrations for an override hiding them.
A reaction role does nothing
Her role has to be above the role being handed out, and she needs Manage Roles. Run /rolemenu view — it flags exactly which roles are out of reach — then /rolemenu resync to restore missing reactions.
Ticket channel wasn't created
She needs Manage Channels, plus permission to create channels inside the chosen category. /ticket type list shows what each type actually resolves to, including inherited defaults.
Welcome messages or autorole aren't firing
Check the channel or role is set (/setup), and that she can post there. For autorole, remember it only ever applies to joins she witnesses — nothing is backfilled.
The autoresponder ignores everything
The channel or the member's role is on the ignore list, or the feature is toggled off. /autoresponder ignore list and /autoresponder view will show which.
Applications aren't posting anywhere
Set a review channel with /application set or /setup — she refuses to take a submission she has nowhere to deliver, rather than losing someone's answers.
A button says "This interaction failed"
Usually a restart mid-click. Press it again. If it persists, check /status to see whether that feature is running.
,G ban does nothing
Check the prefix with /prefix view, and that the mod has the matching Discord permission. If Gemma also didn't react to ,G commands, the Message Content intent isn't enabled for the bot.
Proof didn't get saved
The mod log channel isn't set, or Gemma can't attach files there. /mod view shows the log channel; the case reply says "no mod log channel set" when that's the problem.
A network ban didn't reach my other server
Check /network info in both: the receiving side needs direction receive or both, Gemma needs Ban Members there, and only manual bans sync — honeypot and anti-raid bans deliberately don't.
Someone on the global list is still in my server
Watch-joins only covers people arriving after you enrolled. Run /gban sweep for those already here — and check /gban ignore list in case they're overridden.
Logs say "unknown" for who did it
Gemma doesn't have View Audit Log. Give her the permission and the actor fills in for everything after that.
Anti-nuke / anti-raid / anti-spam did nothing
Check they're actually enabled — the /setup overview shows ⚠️ next to anything that's off. Also: staff and configured staff roles are exempt on purpose, and the whitelist exempts users from anti-nuke.
Jailed member can still see channels
A channel was made after the jail with synced permissions off, or someone edited its overwrites. Run /jail setup again — it repairs every channel — and check the Jail step in /setup, which lists leaks.
Join-to-Create doesn't make a channel
Gemma needs Manage Channels and Move Members, including inside the Voice Channels category. The Voice step in /setup checks both.
Honeypot didn't remove someone
They're staff or exempt, their role sits above hers, or she lacks Ban Members. Your log channel will say which.

Terms & privacy — your side of it

Adding a bot makes you responsible for what it does in your community. Nothing here is onerous, but it's worth five minutes.

Tell your members

Applications and ticket transcripts are stored. A line in your rules channel saying a bot processes messages and keeps what you submit covers most of what people would want to know.

Lock down the log channels

Transcripts, application answers and moderation evidence all land in channels you choose. Staff-only, every time — that's the single biggest privacy decision you'll make here.

Moderation decisions are yours

Gemma performs the ban; your staff decided on it. Appeals come to you, and the case record exists so you can answer them fairly.

Deleting on request

You can remove applications, tickets, skullboard entries and cases with the bot's own commands. If a member asks, you don't need to escalate anywhere.
