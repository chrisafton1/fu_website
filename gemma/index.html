What she does

Nine features. Turn on the ones you want.

Nothing is mandatory and nothing assumes a particular kind of server. Skip the honeypot if you're small, skip applications if you don't recruit staff, skip moderation entirely if another bot already handles it.

👋

Welcome messages

A greeting built from your own embed — title, colour, image, member count, the lot. Preview it privately before anyone sees it, or paste in JSON you designed elsewhere.

📋

Applications

Staff apps without a Google Form. A panel to pick from, questions answered privately in the server, and Accept / Deny buttons for your team. Applicants get a DM with the outcome.

🎫

Tickets

A button that opens a private channel with your support team. Staff can claim it, pull people in, and close it — which saves a full transcript to your log channel.

🎭

Reaction roles

React to grab a role. Group roles together for "pick only one", gate them behind a role, or block them for another. Gemma keeps the menu text and the reactions in sync herself.

💬

Autoresponders

Answer the recurring questions automatically. Match a word anywhere in a message or only exact phrases, reply inline, and use embeds if plain text is too plain.

☠️

Skullboard

A starboard with better taste. Enough ☠️ reactions and the message gets immortalised in its own channel, with a jump link and a live reaction count.

🔰

Autorole

One role, given the moment someone joins. Never re-added if a mod takes it away, and never applied retroactively to people who joined while she was offline.

🔨

Moderation basics

Ban, kick, timeout, warn, purge — as slash commands or ,G ban @user reason. Every action is a numbered case with proof screenshots saved to your mod log.

🍯

Honeypot

A channel real members never post in. Spam bots post everywhere — the moment they touch it they're gone, and your staff get a log entry. Staff are always exempt.

Getting started

Ten minutes, start to finish

  1. Invite her

    One click. Gemma asks only for the permissions her features actually use — no Administrator.

  2. Run /setup

    An interactive menu lists every channel and role a feature wants. Pick what you need, press Do it later for the rest.

  3. Try it

    /welcome test shows the greeting. /application create spins up a staff application. /ticket panel posts the support panel.

  4. Tune it whenever

    Every feature has its own commands, and nothing is locked in. Turn a feature off and its settings wait for you.
